# Strand 0.5.7

- New product images for every connection: realistic renders of braided, coiled USB-C, Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 5, MagSafe and Ethernet cables, plus a charger, USB-C hub, keyboard, mouse and headphones.
- Every image comes in white for light mode and black or space grey for dark mode, now including the hub and headphones. Ethernet uses one image for both.
- Docks show the USB-C hub image, so every hub-like device looks the same; the dock photo is gone.
- No brand logos or lettering appear on any image.
- Product images no longer have a light glow around them in dark mode.
