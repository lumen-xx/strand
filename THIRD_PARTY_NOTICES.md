## Connection images

The images in Sources/Strand/Resources/ConnectionImages are Blender renders made with scripts/render-connection-images.py from the 3D models below, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The models were recoloured, cropped to the parts shown and placed on cables modelled for Strand; maker logos and lettering were removed. The cables, the hub and the MagSafe-style plug are Strand's own geometry.

- USB-C and Thunderbolt cable plugs and the hub's plug: "USB-C Charging Cable (Type-C – Type-C)" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/usb-c-charging-cable-type-c-type-c-39cb1374a9c24cfa815f8efe86a14f61) by alessandroparca (https://sketchfab.com/alessandroparca), CC BY 4.0
- Ethernet plug: "Internet cable | Ethernet | Lan" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/internet-cable-ethernet-lan-167ef9e9ef7c45e89b083e946885d194) by Yan (https://sketchfab.com/yanbatsman), CC BY 4.0
- charger: "iPhone USB-C Charger Box" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/iphone-usb-c-charger-box-ff49b092a14f4f2f8752805dcf511e60) by DesignerKhi (https://sketchfab.com/DesignerKhi), CC BY 4.0
- keyboard: "VortexSeries Mechanical Keyboard GT-8 / NJ80" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/vortexseries-mechanical-keyboard-gt-8-nj80-02d16e9dae72419aa24ec6788318f92b) by Rendy K (https://sketchfab.com/RendyK), CC BY 4.0
- headphones: "Sony wh 1000xm5" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sony-wh-1000xm5-6012e71450874d7594968a607d9fffce) by Angelica_Andreasson (https://sketchfab.com/Angelica_Andreasson), CC BY 4.0
- mouse: "Computer mouse" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/computer-mouse-6e7940d9e2144efeae3468a906f27e07) by zhe_kan (https://sketchfab.com/zhe_kan), CC BY 4.0

No endorsement or identified hardware is implied by the category illustrations.
